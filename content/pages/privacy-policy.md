- heading: What data we collect
  text: |
    **Data you give us**

    If you use our [contact form](/contact.html) or email us, we receive your name, your email address and your message. We use this only to reply to you. We keep it only as long as needed to answer your question.

    Messages from the contact form are delivered to our email inbox by **FormSubmit** (formsubmit.co), a form delivery service.

    **Data collected automatically**

    When you open a page, your browser sends some technical data, such as your **IP address**, browser type, device type and the page you visited. This is needed to show you the website. We do not use this data to identify you.

- heading: Cookies
  text: |
    Cookies are small files that a website saves in your browser.

    - **Necessary cookies:** used to remember your cookie choice. These do not need your permission.
    - **Advertising cookies:** used by Google and its partners to show adverts and measure how they perform. These are **only used if you agree**.

    When you first visit this website, a cookie message asks for your choice. You can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy and cookie settings** button on the website, or by deleting cookies in your browser.

    We do not use analytics cookies at the moment. If we add them, we will update this policy first.
- heading: Advertising
  text: |
    This website uses **Google AdSense** to show adverts. This helps us keep the guide free.

    - Third-party vendors, **including Google**, use cookies to show adverts based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
    - Google's use of advertising cookies lets Google and its partners show adverts to you based on your visits to this website and/or other websites on the internet.
    - In the EU, UK and Switzerland, personalised adverts are only shown **if you agree** in the cookie message. If you do not agree, you may still see adverts, but they are not based on your browsing.

    **How to turn off personalised adverts**

    - Google: visit [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
    - Other advertising companies: visit [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu) (Europe) or [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

    Learn more about [how Google uses data from websites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

- heading: Legal basis
  text: |
    We process data on these legal grounds under the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**:

    - **Legitimate interest:** to run, secure and improve the website.
    - **Consent:** to reply to messages you send through the contact form.
    - **Consent:** for advertising cookies and personalised adverts. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
- heading: Your rights
  text: |
    Under the GDPR you have the right to:

    - **see** the personal data we hold about you
    - **correct** data that is wrong
    - **delete** your data
    - **object to** or **limit** how we use your data
    - **receive** your data in a common format
    - **withdraw your consent** at any time

    To use any of these rights, contact us using the details on the [Legal Notice](/legal-notice.html) page.

    You can also make a complaint to the **Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC)** in Malta at [idpc.org.mt](https://idpc.org.mt).
