- heading: In short
  text: |
    > We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell your data. We do not use analytics or tracking cookies at the moment. Some technical data is processed by the companies that host and support this website, as explained below.
- heading: Who is responsible for your data
  text: |
    The website **maltaexpatguide.com** is run by Malta Expat Guide. Our full details are on the [Legal Notice](/legal-notice.html) page.

    If you have a question about your data, contact us using the details on that page.
- heading: What data we collect
  text: |
    **Data you give us**

    If you email us, we receive your email address, your name (if you give it) and your message. We use this only to reply to you. We keep it only as long as needed to answer your question.

    **Data collected automatically**

    When you open a page, your browser sends some technical data, such as your **IP address**, browser type, device type and the page you visited. This is needed to show you the website. We do not use this data to identify you.
- heading: Services we use
  text: |
    Like most websites, we use a few outside services. They may process your IP address and technical data:

    | Service | What it does | Company |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | GitHub Pages | Hosts the website | GitHub, Inc. (USA) |
    | Google Fonts | Shows the fonts on the website | Google Ireland Ltd. |
    | Unsplash | Shows some photos | Unsplash Inc. (USA) |

    Some of these companies are based in the USA. They are covered by the **EU–US Data Privacy Framework** or use other safeguards approved by the EU.
- heading: Cookies
  text: |
    At the moment, this website **does not set cookies** for tracking, analytics or advertising.

    If we add advertising or analytics in the future, we will update this policy first. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your permission before any non-essential cookies are used. You will be able to change your choice at any time.
- heading: Advertising
  text: |
    In the future, we may show adverts from **Google AdSense** to help keep this guide free.

    If we do, Google and its partners may use cookies to show adverts based on your visits to this and other websites, but **only if you agree** in the cookie message. You can learn more and turn off personalised adverts at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) and read [how Google uses data from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- heading: Affiliate and referral links
  text: |
    Some links on this website are referral links, for example to Revolut. When you click one, you leave our website. The other company then collects data under its own privacy policy. We may learn that someone signed up through our link, but we do not receive your personal details.
- heading: Links to other websites
  text: |
    Our guides link to many other websites, such as government offices and service providers. We are not responsible for how those websites handle your data. Please read their privacy policies.
- heading: Legal basis
  text: |
    We process data on these legal grounds under the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**:

    - **Legitimate interest:** to run, secure and improve the website, and to reply to your messages.
    - **Consent:** for any non-essential cookies, such as advertising cookies, if we add them.
- heading: Your rights
  text: |
    Under the GDPR you have the right to:

    - **see** the personal data we hold about you
    - **correct** data that is wrong
    - **delete** your data
    - **object to** or **limit** how we use your data
    - **receive** your data in a common format
    - **withdraw your consent** at any time

    To use any of these rights, contact us using the details on the [Legal Notice](/legal-notice.html) page.

    You can also make a complaint to the **Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC)** in Malta at [idpc.org.mt](https://idpc.org.mt).
- heading: Children
  text: |
    This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.
- heading: Changes to this policy
  text: |
    We may update this policy, for example when we add new features. The date below shows the latest version.
