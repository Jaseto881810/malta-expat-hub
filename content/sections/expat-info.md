  Moving to a new country means a lot of paperwork. This section explains the rules that apply to you as a foreign national in Malta, in simple steps.

  **Where to start:**

  - **New to Malta?** Read [Permits and IDs](/expat-info/permits-ids.html) first. It explains visas, work permits and the e-Residence card.
  - **Starting a job?** Read [Employment in Malta](/expat-info/employment-in-malta.html) and [Taxation](/expat-info/taxation.html) to understand your contract, salary and tax.
  - **Need a doctor?** [Healthcare](/expat-info/healthcare.html) explains public and private care, and who can use the free state system.
  - **Moving with children?** See [Raising Children in Malta](/expat-info/raising-children.html) and [Benefits and Allowances](/expat-info/benefits-allowances.html).

  The guides also cover driving licences, sending money abroad, consumer rights, the police and voting. Rules change often, so each guide shows when it was last updated.
