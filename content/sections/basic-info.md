  Malta is small, but it has its own language, food, traditions and way of life. This section helps you understand everyday Malta and feel at home faster.

  **Where to start:**

  - **Language:** Maltese and English are both official languages. Read [Languages](/basic-info/languages.html) to learn a few useful Maltese words.
  - **Daily life:** [Local Etiquette](/basic-info/etiquette.html) and [Maltese Customs](/basic-info/customs.html) explain greetings, dress codes, the village festa and family life.
  - **Food and drink:** try pastizzi, ftira and Kinnie with our [Local Food and Snacks](/basic-info/food-snacks.html) and [Local Drinks](/basic-info/drinks.html) guides.
  - **Plan your year:** Malta has 14 [Public Holidays](/basic-info/holidays.html), more than most countries in Europe.

  You will also find simple guides to religion, politics, sport and the local news, so you can follow what is happening around you.
