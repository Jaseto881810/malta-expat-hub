overview: |-
  Malta has state, church and independent schools, and most children learn in both English and Maltese. This section explains the options at every age.

  **Where to start:**

  - **Babies and toddlers:** [Nursery Schools](/education/nursery-schools.html) explains childcare and the Free Childcare Scheme for working parents.
  - **Ages 5 to 11:** [Primary Schools](/education/primary-schools.html) compares state, church and independent schools.
  - **Ages 11 to 16:** [High Schools](/education/high-schools.html) covers secondary school and exams.
  - **English curriculum or the IB?** See [International Schools](/education/international-schools.html).
  - **Adults:** [Higher Education](/education/higher-education.html) covers the University of Malta and MCAST, and [Language Schools](/education/language-schools.html) lists English courses.

  State schools are free for residents, including books and school transport. Places at church schools are usually given by a draw (ballot), so apply early.
