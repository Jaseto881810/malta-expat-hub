    text: |-
      | Date | What to pay or file | Who |
      | --- | --- | --- |
      | **15 March** | Article 11 VAT yearly declaration (for last year) | Small businesses on Article 11 |

      | **30 June** | Tax return for last year, and pay any tax still owed | Self-employed and people with other income |
      | **31 August** | Provisional tax, 2nd part (**30%**) + Class 2, 2nd part | Self-employed |
      | **21 December** | Provisional tax, 3rd part (**50%**) + Class 2, 3rd part | Self-employed |
      | **Every 3 months** | VAT return, about 6 weeks after the end of each period | Businesses on Article 10 |

      If a date falls on a weekend or public holiday, check the MTCA website for the exact deadline.

  - heading: Income tax (provisional tax)
    text: |-
      Self-employed people do not have tax taken from a salary. Instead, they pay **provisional tax** in three parts during the year: 20% in April, 30% in August and 50% in December.

      - The amount is based on your **last tax return**. The MTCA sends you a notice.
      - By **30 June** of the next year, you file your **tax return** and pay the **difference** if you owe more.
      - If you paid too much, you get a **refund**.
      - In your **first year**, you usually have no provisional tax. Save money for your first tax bill.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: VAT
    text: |-
      - **Article 10** (standard VAT): file a **VAT return every 3 months** and pay the VAT you owe at the same time. The due date is about 6 weeks after each period ends. Your exact periods are on your VAT certificate.
      - **Article 11** (small business): no VAT returns. File **one declaration a year**, by **15 March**.
      - **Article 12** (EU services and purchases): may need a short return each month or quarter when you buy or sell services across the EU.

      Read more in [Being Self-Employed](/work-business/self-employed.html).
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Social security (Class 2)
    text: |-
      Class 2 is paid with provisional tax, in three parts: **30 April, 31 August and 21 December**. See the rates in [Social Security for the Self-Employed](/work-business/self-employed-social-security.html).
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Companies and employers
    text: |-
      - **Company tax return:** usually due **9 months after the end of the financial year** (for a calendar year, by 30 September of the next year). Companies also pay provisional tax during the year.
      - **Employers:** pay the tax and social security taken from salaries **every month**, by the end of the following month (form FS5). Send the yearly FS3 to employees and the FS7 to the MTCA in February.
      - **Malta Business Registry:** file the company annual return each year, on the anniversary of the company's registration.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Paying late
    text: |-
      - Late tax has **interest of 0.6% per month**.
      - Late VAT returns and payments have **fines and interest**.
      - Late social security can lower your pension and benefits.

      > Put these dates in your calendar now. Many self-employed people set aside **25–30%** of every payment they receive for tax and social security.
