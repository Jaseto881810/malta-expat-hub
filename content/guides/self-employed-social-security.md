  - heading: What is Class 2?
    text: |-
      Everyone who works in Malta pays **social security** (often called NI). It pays for your **state pension**, sick pay, maternity benefit and other benefits.

      - **Employees** pay **Class 1**. The employer takes it from the salary and pays the same amount again.
      - **Self-employed people** pay **Class 2** themselves.

      Small business owners who work in their own company and take a salary usually pay Class 1 through the company. Sole traders and partners pay Class 2.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Class 2 rates for 2026
    text: |-
      Class 2 is based on your **net profit** (income minus business costs). Most people pay based on their profit from the **year before**.

      | Your yearly net profit | You pay per week |
      | --- | --- |
      | Up to **€12,543.72** | **€36.18** (fixed) |
      | **€12,543.73 – €29,083.35** | **15%** of your profit |
      | **€29,083.36 or more** | **€83.89** (maximum) |

      These rates are for people born on or after 1 January 1962. If you were born before 1962, the maximum is lower: €73.56 a week from €25,500.

      **Example:** your profit is €20,000. You pay 15% = €3,000 a year, about €57.69 a week.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Special cases
    text: |-
      - **Employee with a side business:** you pay Class 1 on your salary. You may also need to pay Class 2 on your self-employed profit. Ask the Contributions section of the MTCA.
      - **Very low income:** if your profit is under €910 a year, you usually do not pay Class 2.
      - **Students, pensioners and some part-time workers** may pay 15% of their profit instead of the fixed minimum.
      - **Paying less than the minimum** is possible in some cases, but it can lower your future pension.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: When and how to pay
    text: |-
      Class 2 is paid to the **Malta Tax and Customs Administration (MTCA)** in three parts, on the same dates as provisional tax:

      | Date | Payment |
      | --- | --- |
      | **30 April** | 1st part |
      | **31 August** | 2nd part |
      | **21 December** | 3rd part |

      You receive a payment notice. You can pay online with the MTCA. See all dates in [Payment Dates for VAT, Tax and Social Security](/work-business/tax-payment-dates.html).
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Why it matters
    text: |-
      - Your contributions build your **Maltese state pension**. Missing years can lower your pension.
      - Contributions paid in Malta can count together with years in other EU countries for your pension.
      - You need paid contributions to claim benefits such as **sickness** and **maternity** benefit.

      > Rates change every January. This page shows the 2026 rates. For your own case, ask the MTCA Contributions section or an accountant.
