blocks:
  - heading: What is the Nomad Residence Permit?
    text: |-
      The **Nomad Residence Permit** lets people from outside the EU live in Malta while they **work remotely** for a company or clients abroad. It is run by the **Residency Malta Agency**.

      You can work from Malta, but **you cannot work for Maltese companies or clients**.

      > EU, EEA and Swiss citizens do not need this permit. They can live and work in Malta freely. See [Residence Cards](/work-business/residence-cards.html).
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Who can apply?
    text: |-
      You can apply if you are a **non-EU citizen** and you:

      - work for an employer registered **outside Malta**, or
      - own a business registered **outside Malta**, or
      - work as a **freelancer** for clients **outside Malta**.

      You must earn at least **€42,000 a year** (gross), which is about €3,500 a month.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: What you need
    text: |-
      - **Passport** valid for the whole stay
      - **Proof of remote work:** a work contract, company papers or freelance contracts
      - **Proof of income:** payslips or bank statements showing at least €42,000 a year
      - **Health insurance** that covers you in Malta
      - **A home in Malta:** a rental contract or property you own
      - **Police conduct certificate** from your home country
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Costs, length and renewals
    text: |-
      | | |
      | --- | --- |
      | **Application fee** | €300 per person (main applicant and each family member) |
      | **Residence card** | Small extra fee to Identità per person |
      | **How long?** | 1 year |
      | **Renewals** | Up to 3 times, so up to **4 years** in total |
      | **To renew** | You must have lived in Malta for at least 5 months in the last 12 months |
      | **Family** | Your partner and children can join you |

      You apply online on the Residency Malta Agency nomad website. Apply a few months before you want to arrive.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Tax for digital nomads
    text: |-
      - **First 12 months:** your remote work income is usually **not taxed in Malta**, as long as you are not treated as a Malta tax resident.
      - **After 12 months:** your remote work income is taxed at a flat **10%** in Malta.
      - Other income, such as local rent, is taxed at normal Malta rates.

      You may still have to file tax in your home country, for example if you are a US citizen. Ask a tax adviser about your own situation.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Good to know
    text: |-
      - The Nomad permit **does not lead** to permanent residence or citizenship.
      - Rent is often the biggest cost. Check prices with our [Rent Price Checker](/tools/rent-prices.html) and plan your budget with the [Cost of Living Calculator](/tools/cost-of-living.html).
      - Malta has good co-working spaces in Sliema, St Julian's, Gżira and Valletta.
