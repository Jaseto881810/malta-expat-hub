  - heading: Which route is right for you?
    text: |-
      There is no single "Malta visa". The right route depends on **where you are from** and **why you want to live here**.

      - **EU, EEA or Swiss citizen?** You can live and work in Malta freely. You only need to register and get an e-Residence card. See [Residence Cards](/work-business/residence-cards.html).
      - **Non-EU and you have a job in Malta?** You usually need a **Single Permit** through your employer. See [Permits and IDs](/expat-info/permits-ids.html).
      - **Non-EU and you work remotely for a foreign company?** Look at the **Nomad Residence Permit**. See [Digital Nomads](/work-business/digital-nomads.html).
      - **You want to move with your own money, not a job?** Look at the programmes on this page.

      | Programme | Who it is for | Main costs (2026) | Tax |
      | --- | --- | --- | --- |
      | **Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP)** | Non-EU people who want to live in Malta for good | €99,000 in fees, plus a home to buy or rent | Normal Malta tax rules |
      | **Global Residence Programme (GRP)** | Non-EU people with income from abroad | €6,000 fee, plus a home to buy or rent | 15% on foreign income you bring to Malta, at least €15,000 a year |
      | **The Residence Programme (TRP)** | EU, EEA and Swiss citizens with income from abroad | Similar to the GRP | Similar to the GRP |

      | **Nomad Residence Permit** | Non-EU remote workers | €300 fee | See [Digital Nomads](/work-business/digital-nomads.html) |

      > Malta no longer sells citizenship. The citizenship-by-investment route closed in 2025 after a ruling of the EU Court of Justice.

  - heading: Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP)
    text: |-
      The MPRP gives non-EU citizens and their family **permanent residence** in Malta. It is run by the **Residency Malta Agency**, and you must apply through a licensed agent.

      **What you need (2026):**

      - **Capital:** €500,000 (with at least €150,000 in cash or investments), or €650,000 (with at least €75,000 in cash or investments).
      - **A home for at least 5 years:** buy a property worth at least **€375,000**, or rent one for at least **€14,000 a year**.
      - **Fees:** €60,000 administration fee + €37,000 government contribution + €2,000 donation to a Maltese charity = **€99,000**. Each extra adult family member costs €7,500.
      - **Clean record and health insurance** for the whole family.

      You can usually get a one-year temporary permit while your application is checked. There is no minimum number of days you must spend in Malta each year.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Global Residence Programme and The Residence Programme
    text: |-
      These two programmes are mainly about **tax**. They are for people who live on income from abroad, such as investments or a foreign business.

      - **GRP** is for non-EU citizens. **TRP** is for EU, EEA and Swiss citizens.
      - Foreign income you bring into Malta is taxed at a flat **15%**. You pay at least **€15,000** tax a year.
      - **Home:** buy a property worth at least **€275,000** (€220,000 in Gozo or the south), or rent for at least **€9,600 a year** (€8,750 in Gozo or the south).
      - **Fee:** €6,000 (€5,500 for a home in Gozo or the south).

      > **Change from 2027:** from 1 January 2027 these programmes join a new *Individual Tax Programme*, with higher minimum tax and property amounts. Applications sent by 31 December 2026 can keep the current rules for some years. Ask a licensed adviser if you are thinking of applying.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Malta Retirement Programme
    text: This programme is for **pensioners** from any country. Your pension brought into Malta is taxed at **15%**, with a minimum tax of **€7,500 a year** (plus €500 for each dependant). You need a home that meets the same property amounts as the GRP, and your pension must be at least 75% of your income.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Good to know
    text: |-
      - **Use a licensed agent.** The MPRP and the tax programmes must go through an approved agent or registered mandatary. Check they are licensed before you pay anything.
      - **Rules change often.** Amounts and conditions change with new laws. Always check the latest rules with the Residency Malta Agency or the Malta Tax and Customs Administration (MTCA).
      - **Residence is not the same as tax.** Having a residence card does not always make you a tax resident. Read our [Taxation](/expat-info/taxation.html) guide.
