  - heading: What is the e-Residence card?
    text: |-
      The **e-Residence card** is your Malta ID card as a foreign resident. It shows your name, photo and your right to live in Malta. It is issued by **Identità**, the government agency for ID cards and residence.

      You need it if you stay in Malta for **more than 3 months**. You also need it for many everyday things: opening a bank account, getting a tax number, using public healthcare and getting a free Tallinja bus card.

  - heading: EU and non-EU citizens
    text: |-
      | | EU, EEA and Swiss citizens | Non-EU citizens |
      | --- | --- | --- |
      | **Do I need a permit first?** | No. You have the right to live here | Yes. The card comes with your permit (for example a Single Permit or Nomad permit) |
      | **What is the card based on?** | Work, self-employment, study, or enough money and health insurance | The permit you were given |
      | **How long is it valid?** | Usually **5 years** | Usually the same as your permit (often 1 year) |
      | **After 5 years** | You can apply for **permanent residence** | You may be able to apply for long-term residence |

  - heading: How to apply, step by step
    text: |-
      1. **Find a home and register the lease.** Your rental contract must be registered with the Housing Authority. This is your proof of address. See our [Renting](/accommodation/renting.html) guide.
      2. **Get your documents ready** (see the list below).
      3. **Apply to Identità.** EU citizens apply on the Identità Expatriates website with the right form (for example the form for employment or self-employment). Non-EU citizens apply as part of their permit.
      4. **Book an appointment.** Go to the Identità office in person to give your photo, signature and, for non-EU citizens, fingerprints.
      5. **Collect your card.** Identità tells you when the card is ready. Bring your passport when you collect it.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Documents you usually need
    text: |-
      - Valid **passport** or national ID card
      - **Registered lease** or proof of where you live
      - **Proof of why you are in Malta**, for example:
        - **Employee:** your work contract and the Jobsplus engagement form
        - **Self-employed:** your Jobsplus self-employed registration and VAT number
        - **Student:** a letter from your school or university
        - **Not working:** proof of enough money and health insurance
      - Passport photos, if asked

      The exact list depends on your situation. Check the form on the Identità website before your appointment, so you do not need to come back.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
  - heading: Changing your address or losing your card
    text: |-
      - **New address?** Tell Identità when you move. Fill in the change of address form and bring your new registered lease.
      - **Lost or stolen card?** Report it to the police first. Then apply for a new card at Identità and bring the police report.
      - **Card expiring?** Apply to renew before it expires. Non-EU citizens should renew their permit about 2 months before it ends.
    html: ''
    custom: ''
