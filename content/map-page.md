description: Interactive map of Malta and Gozo - things to do, hiking trails, dive sites, beaches, neighbourhoods with rent levels, expat services and English schools.
overview: |-
  This free map brings together the places expats ask about most, in one view. Choose a category at the top, or search for a town or place name.

  - **Things to do:** sights, museums, markets, viewpoints and family days out.
  - **Hiking trails:** start points and route lines for popular walks. Download a trail as a GPX file for your phone. The best season is October to May.
  - **Diving:** popular dive sites with depth and level.
  - **Beaches:** sandy beaches and rocky swimming spots in Malta, Gozo and Comino.
  - **Neighbourhoods:** the boundaries of each area, with a rough rent level, to help you choose where to live.
  - **Expat services:** offices you will need, such as Identità, Jobsplus and the Housing Authority.
  - **English schools:** language schools for adults.

  Places are shown in their approximate position. Opening times, prices and conditions can change, so please check before you go. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
